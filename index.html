About

I am a Doctor of Philosophy in Applied Mathematics from the University of Waterloo. My research focuses on scientific machine learning, kernel-based approximation, numerical partial differential equations, variational inequalities, and graph representation learning.

My work combines applied mathematics, scientific computing, and machine learning to develop computational methods for partial differential equations, free-boundary problems, inverse problems, and high-dimensional variational problems.

Research Interests

  • Scientific Machine Learning
  • Numerical Methods for Partial Differential Equations
  • Radial Basis Function Networks and Kernel Methods
  • Variational Inequalities and Free-Boundary Problems
  • Bayesian Inverse Problems
  • Graph Representation Learning and Graph Neural Networks
  • Topological Data Analysis
