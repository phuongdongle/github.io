About

I am a Doctor of Philosophy in Applied Mathematics from the University of Waterloo. My research focuses on numerical methods, scientific machine learning, kernel-based approximation, and computational approaches for partial differential equations and variational problems.

My work lies at the intersection of applied mathematics, machine learning, scientific computing, and data-driven modeling. I am especially interested in developing scalable approximation methods for high-dimensional problems, free-boundary problems, and inverse problems arising in science and engineering.

Research Interests

  • Numerical Methods for Partial Differential Equations
  • Variational Inequalities and Free-Boundary Problems

  • Topological Data Analysis
